# Techniques, terms, papers

What each process at TeleportPress 時分印刷 does, what it can't do, and the words the studio uses. Sources: the studio's guides at https://teleportpress.com/guides (紙材與油墨, 印刷與加工, 凸版原理與效果, 設計規範, 版數計算), its facts page and its order form, as of 2026-11-03. Which papers and foil colours are available changes: read `live-sources.md` before listing them.

## 凸版印刷 letterpress

- Best at: text, line art, simple graphics, illustrations in a few flat colours, where the impression and the paper's texture show.
- The impression depth is set at the press: from barely there to deep. Thick, soft papers take the deepest bite. Double-sided cards get a gentler bite on one side so it doesn't show through the other; artwork that doesn't overlap front to back allows deeper bites on both.
- **Inks are transparent.** Light ink on dark paper prints dull and murky (yellow on red looks muddy). White ink on dark or grey paper prints as a blotchy light grey, even printed twice. For white or light artwork on dark paper, use 霧銀箔 matte silver foil (sharpest, best for small text), 白箔 white foil (true white, text 9 pt or more with a little extra spacing), or metallic silver ink (a soft silver-grey that keeps the impression; small text reads grey).
- **Dark papers** are the black and brown paper families; the studio's paper pages mark them 深色紙.
- **Metallic inks** look closer to brown (gold) and grey (silver) than to metal. For real shine, use foil.
- **Large solid areas** (one area covering half the face or more) print uneven and mottled ("snowflaked") on the studio's uncoated papers, and can mark the sheet behind. Instead: a coloured paper with the art printed or foiled on it, or a coloured sheet duplexed to a white one. Knockout details inside a large pressed area don't stand up; they press down with it.
- **Gradients, photos, watercolour**: not by letterpress. A small part of a design can become a halftone (see `print-file.md` §6) or flat shapes; a full-colour piece needs 數位印刷 or 合版印刷 (`full-colour.md`).
- Custom-mixed ink colours are possible; the studio's standard inks avoid a mixing charge. Very light inks (e.g. Pantone 9043 U) can vary 5–10% from the mixed colour.

## 燙金 foil stamping

- A flat, glossy (or matte) metal or colour film pressed slightly into the paper. Crisp, opaque, works on dark papers.
- White foil is a powder foil with slightly soft edges; matte silver MS-01 is sharper.
- Large foil areas need more heat and pressure: fine text nearby can fill in, thin papers can curl, textured papers can show pinholes.
- **Foil, then emboss the same spot**: not advised on large foil areas (燙金 glossary: 大面積燙箔後不建議再打凸). For any foil and emboss on the same spot, ask the studio first.
- Each foil colour is its own plate. A rarer foil can add a sourcing cost; the current colour list is a live read.

## 打凸 emboss / 打凹 deboss

- Male and female dies squeeze the paper. 打凸 raises the front and dents the back; 打凹 does the opposite. Keep text and art on the other side away from the affected area.
- Shows best on light-coloured papers (it reads by light and shadow) and thick stock (300 gsm and up; 400 gsm+ for large shapes; a deep deboss with no mark behind wants 450–570 gsm).
- For logos, marks, borders and patterns, not body text.
- Deep emboss or male/female dies can crack the foil layer of gold or silver card stock.
- A 對裱 duplex card hides the dent on the back.

## 紙邊上色 edge painting

- Colour sprayed or brushed onto the cut edges of thick cards (350 gsm and up, or duplexed cards). Straight edges only: not on rounded corners or die-cut shapes.
- Can't be combined with emboss: the clamping flattens it.
- Pre-mixed colours from a fixed list, no custom colour matching.

## 對裱 duplex, 刀模軋型 die-cutting, 烤松香 thermography

- **對裱**: two or three sheets glued into one thick card: more weight, a different colour each side, a coloured core showing at the edge, a hidden emboss dent. The order form takes up to three sheets.
- **刀模軋型**: custom shapes, rounded corners, holes. About 1 mm tolerance; keep borders 3–5 mm from the cut. Holes are cut by a die too.
- **烤松香**: raised glossy type from resin powder; softer edges than letterpress; not for hairline type or white and fluorescent inks. Ask for it in the form's 整體備註.

## Plate counting (版數)

1. Front and back are counted separately.
2. One plate per ink colour. A blind deboss (no ink or clear ink) is a plate too; so is each foil colour.
3. Identical content across several designs, in the same place and colour, can share a plate (共版).
4. Add them up. Example: three employee cards, fronts all black → 1 plate; backs each with one accent colour (orange, red, blue) plus shared black contact details → 4 plates; total 5. Without the shared black it would be 7.

Fewer plates cost less: one ink plus paper colour often looks better than many inks. Same ink on both sides saves a colour mix but still needs two plates.

## Papers

- Read the live guide (https://teleportpress.com/guides/paper-ink) for the recommended papers; for an actual pick, the order form's paper list or 「請幫我推薦」 (recommend for me), or the paper sample pages at https://teleportpress.com/paper-swatches.
- Weight guide: 116–240 g envelopes and sleeves (120 g closes flatter), not for large foil or emboss; 250–300 g common cards with limited depth; 300–400 g recommended for name cards and invitations; 400 g+ for deep impressions, edge painting and large foil areas.
- Papers over 250 g crack when folded.
- **Never 美國啤酒紙 on cards or postcards: it curls.** For a beer-board look, offer 薪傳瑪芬紙 or 日本啤酒紙.
- Rough papers make white ink blotchy; hard-core papers (e.g. MATTER 美奈特) can show the impression on the back.
- Customers may supply their own paper (「我會自備紙材」 on the form): the studio needs extra sheets and doesn't guarantee results on outside stock. Ask the studio for the cut size and quantity.

## What the studio does and doesn't do

- In house: 凸版印刷, 燙金, 打凸／打凹, 紙邊上色, 對裱, 刀模軋型.
- 數位印刷 / 合版印刷 (photos, gradients, CMYK artwork) usually go to another printer. The studio takes them on, at an extra charge, when the customer wants its careful file check, or when the piece also needs 燙金, 打凸／打凹 or 模切: see `full-colour.md`.
- Prices on the site are production only: layout and design are separate, paid services.
- Photographs and full-colour art can't be printed by letterpress itself; the studio can advise whether letterpress suits a design.
- Post-press only jobs (foil or edge paint on prints made elsewhere): email the studio first.
